Learning cloud engineering takes practice. You do not need to understand
everything on your first pass. Pick a learning path, work through it, and
return to difficult ideas as you need them.

## Choose a path

It is easy to spend a study session comparing courses instead of learning.
For now, use Learn to Cloud as your main path. Write down questions about
other resources and return to them after you finish a topic.

You can change your plan when it no longer meets your needs. The goal is to
make a deliberate choice, not to restart whenever you see a new roadmap.

## Make room to focus

Choose a place and time where you can work without frequent interruptions.
Before a session, put your phone out of reach or silence notifications.
Close browser tabs that are not needed for the task. If a social feed pulls
your attention away, try a browser feed blocker or sign out during study.

Use the settings that work for your life. You do not need to remove every
app or be available for study at all times.

## Practice and review

Reading or watching can introduce an idea. Practice helps you use it.
After a short section, close the material and explain the idea in your own
words. Try a small exercise. If you get stuck, inspect the result, ask a
specific question, and try again. Return to the idea later to check what
you remember.

Breaks are part of learning. A regular, manageable routine is easier to
maintain than an occasional long session that leaves you exhausted.

## Make a study plan

1. Pick two or three times this week when you can study.
2. Choose one small task for each session, such as reading a lesson and
   trying its example.
3. Decide what you will do to reduce distractions.
4. At the end of each session, note what you learned and your next question.

Adjust the plan when you learn what fits your schedule.
